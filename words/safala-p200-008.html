ሰፍለ*
safala
Verbmulti-derivation: A1, T2, S.
Related forms
A1 አስፈለ
to place the vessel for collecting the flour near the mill
T2 ትሰፈለ
to be provided with a ስፋል (mill)
S. ስፋል m., pl. አስፍለት, አስፍለት
1. vessel made of palm-wattle for collecting the flour while grinding; 2. palm-mat with an opening in its centre, used by women for the vapour-bath
