ጸረ
ṣara
VerbO1/O3/T2/T3
1.to call aloud, to cry
2.to bind up the legs and the neck of a skin (in order to make a water-bag), to bind up the udder (to hinder the young animal as it sucks)
3.to make ready (the tobacco-pipe)
4.to begin to bloom (corn)
Related forms
O3 ጸረረ
1. to be refractory, to anger; 2. to set an ambush; 3. to tie up the udder; 4. to grow (field, durra)

to be tied up
