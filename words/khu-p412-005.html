kḥu
NounS., m.
a bird; according to P IV A, 463 apparently a kind of magpie, black-and-white in colour; according to P II, 240, note, a small bird not zoologically identifiable further; according to Lt a small, black bird that always follows the raven, though chased away by it; hence one says of an intrusive person ክቹ፡ገአ፡ዲብዬ; according to RB 230 the Abyssinian hornbill, Buceros coronatus
