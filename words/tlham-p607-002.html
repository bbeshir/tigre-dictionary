ጥልሓም
ṭəlḥām
NounS.
1.disease of cattle (poisoning caused by grass or by water on the place of a fire in a forest)
2.flesh of cattle suffering from this disease
Etymology: Ar. tulhūm aqua corrupta et foetens (Freytag)
