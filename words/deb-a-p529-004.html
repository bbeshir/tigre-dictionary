ደብአ
deb'a
VerbO1
to close, to lock
Related forms
እፍ፡ደብእ
to close somebody's mouth, to silence (v.t.)
Part.a. ዳብእ
he has closed the door of something, i.e. he is the right man for something
Part.p. ድቡእ, f. ድብእ(ት)
closed, covered
እብ፡ድብእ
without saying anything, silent
2. (von einem Schwert) ohne Zeichnung
(of a sword) without design
Part.a. O2 ደብእ
one who closes, locks
S. መድብእ, pl. መዳብእ
lock of a door made of thorny bushes, bolt, cover
ምድባእ (beim Schwert)
(on the sword) place without design
