gamḥa
VerbO1
1.to kneel down
2.to resist (camel when being loaded)
Related forms
O3 ጋምሐ
to curtail, to blunt (horns of a bull)
Part. p. O3 ጉሙሕ (Stier)
(bull) with clipped horns
AT3 አትጋምሕ
to fall on one's knees, not to yield, to persist
