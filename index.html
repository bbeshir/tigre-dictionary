About this dictionary

This site presents 10,095 Tigre headwords, transcribed in Ge'ez script with transliteration.

Only English glosses are shown. Where a headword has no recorded English gloss (for example, cross-reference entries that simply point to another headword), the entry says so plainly.

Grammatical codes (such as O1, S., Adj.) reproduce traditional stem-class notation and are grouped into broader part-of-speech categories for filtering.
